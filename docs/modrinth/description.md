*Every portal is a wound. Every wound remembers.*

**Extreme Dimensions** is a survival-horror overhaul of everything beyond the Overworld. The Nether and the End become
timed expeditions on a physical Tether. A fourth world, **the Seam**, waits behind a needle's tear. An ancient tribe
watches from the dark. Four Elders, a Starved One and an ending that changes your world for good, and after it, a
tapestry you weave yourself.

Every death leaves a mark. Nothing here is forgiven for free.

---

## ⛓ Expeditions on a Tether

1. **Charge an anchor**: a lodestone beside a portal, fed with fuel. It becomes an **Ember Anchor** for the Nether or
   a **Void Anchor** for the End.
2. **Bind your Tether** to it. A real chain lies on the ground wherever you walk.
3. **Step through.** Your time is the charge *you* stored, shown as a boss bar, and it drains faster the further you
   wander from the portal.
4. **Come home** before it runs out. If you don't, you suffer a **Collapse**, wake at home, and choose one of three
   curses.

## ☠ 92 curses

Permanent, stacking curses in four sets (*Beyond the wound*, *Overworld*, *Everywhere* and the cryptic *Extreme* set),
and beneath them **24 Abyssal curses**, each broken only by a deed: slay undead in daylight, offer a Nether Star, stay
awake through three nights...

Lift them through **atonement** earned in the wild, **Tether Fragments**, a **Mending Cloth**, or by **unweaving** a
full set at the Weaver's Loom in a fight against its knots. Every stack lifted leaves a scar. No farm can do it for
you: spawner rooms and mob grinders earn nothing.

On Hard and Extreme, curses you have never carried show only in the **Seam script**, an alphabet of the mod's own.

## 🌌 The Seam and the Fray

Use a **Seam Needle** on crying obsidian and step into a world between dimensions: floating islands under a sky of
nebulae, colossal spires, Weaver looms, villages of the Thinned (who only move when you are not looking), and Veil
Rays gliding overhead. You have five minutes before the Seam throws you home.

Wells at the islands' edges drop into **the Fray**, its deeper layer, where time runs out twice as fast and the rays
hunt.

## 🩸 The tribe, the Elders and the story

- **The tribe** watches the Overworld. Lurkers steal and flee to their hoards, the Keeper guards them, and Hollowlings
  can be befriended. Win their standing from *hunted* to *kin*.
- **Four Elders**: the Matriarch, the Ferryman, the Last Weaver and the Knot Mother, with arenas raised by flying
  blocks, domain phases, warding cores and falling islands. Beyond them waits the **Starved One**.
- **Thirty journal pages** of a nameless Wanderer surface as you play and lead you to the ending. There you **Mend**
  the first wound or **Sever** the Tether, and your world stays changed.
- **The Unfinished World**: weave a six-panel **tapestry** from threads earned by great deeds, each a lasting boon,
  then hold your loom through **the Weaving**. A finished tapestry offers **New Thread+**, a harder thread for the
  whole world.

## ⚒ And much more

- **Tetherite**: a tier past netherite, with tools, a spear and armour that return from the void
- **20 enchantments** that reach into the mod's own workings, and two new armour trims
- **10 Relics** with a gift and a price, **Pacts** for willing risk, and weekly **Rift Trials** with medals
- **Co-op**: revive fallen friends, carry their curses, share anchors
- **55 advancements**, a bestiary, a ledger and a guided first expedition
- A **music disc** and its own title and loading screens with music

## ⚙ Difficulty and settings

Four presets: **Easy**, **Normal**, **Hard** and **Extreme**. Every number in the mod can also be changed, in
`config/extremedimensions.json` or in the in-game settings screen (through Mod Menu). Operators change the server's
values from the same screen.

## 🖥 For servers

- **TAB placeholders**: with [Text Placeholder API](https://modrinth.com/mod/placeholder-api) installed, 20
  `%extremedimensions:…%` placeholders show curses, status, time left, the ending and more in your tablist.
- **Stats export**: a JSON file for a web page with the world and every player.
- **Discord notifications**: Elders defeated, the ending and the tapestry, posted to a channel through a webhook.

## 📦 Installation

- **Minecraft 26.3**, **Fabric Loader 0.19.5+**, **Fabric API** (Java 25)
- Install on the **server and every client**
- Play a normal **Survival** world, not Hardcore: Hardcore makes you a spectator on death, and the mod gives death its
  own consequences instead

**Works with** Sodium, Iris, Mod Menu and Text Placeholder API, all tested in CI on every build.

**Languages:** English and Dutch.
